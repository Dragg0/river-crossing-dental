Interview note: this session had no structured question tool. Facts below are taken from the existing site and from Dr. Peterson's review of the marketing draft. Nothing here is a new clinical claim.

## Platform

web

## Users

People in and around Riverton, Utah looking for a family dentist. Many are on a phone. Two situations matter on the pages in this draft: someone with tooth pain who needs to call or book today, and an adult or a parent of a teen considering clear aligners.

## Product Purpose

The public website for River Crossing Family Dental & Orthodontics. It explains the care the office already offers and gives a clear way to call 801-254-8812 or book online.

## Positioning

A family practice in Riverton that does general dentistry, cosmetic care, implants, orthodontics, and same-day emergency visits, with the offers and facts already published on the site.

## Operating Context

Office hours are Monday through Thursday. New patients can call Eve, the AI receptionist, at night and on weekends to schedule. Appointments are still during office hours. Booking opens the existing Modento page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Phone display 801-254-8812, tel:+18012548812. Booking is https://book.modento.io/river-crossing-dental.
- Published offers: $49 emergency exam and X-ray for new patients (treatment after the exam is not included), $89 cleaning exam and X-ray, and a complimentary orthodontic consultation for new patients (treatment and appliances are not included).
- Do not add testimonials, patient names, provider tiers, certifications, prices, or results that are not already on the site.
- The reviews page is approved. Leave it alone.

## Brand Commitments

The practice name is River Crossing Family Dental & Orthodontics. The voice already on the site is warm and plain. Visual identity stays the navy, cream, and coral already in the stylesheet.

## Evidence on Hand

Copy, offers, reviews, photos, and the Google rating (4.7 from 345+ reviews) live in `src/content` and `src/lib/site-config.ts`. Do not invent quotes or ratings.

## Product Principles

- Say what the office already does, in plain language.
- On an emergency visit, the phone number and the $49 exam are easy to find.
- On Invisalign, adults and teens can understand the consult, the wear, and the limits of the offer without a sales pitch.
- New pages should look like the rest of this website.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Pages are used on phones. Call and book controls need to be easy to tap. The site already has an accessibility statement at `/website-accessibility`.
